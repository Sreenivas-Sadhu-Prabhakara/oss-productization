Vibe-Trading productization — a market research copilot

Vibe-Trading is a multi-agent AI research workspace for markets: plain-English questions become strategies, analysis and backtests. Productize it as a vertical copilot for one exchange — and let its "research, not a bot" framing keep you the right side of the regulator.

What it is

A research workspace, explicitly not an auto-trader.

Built by HKU's Data Intelligence Lab, Vibe-Trading turns natural-language prompts into market strategies, analysis and backtests. It's Python + FastAPI with a React front end, and it exposes a set of MCP tools any MCP client can call; only the multi-agent "swarm" feature needs an LLM key. Crucially, the project frames itself as a research assistant, not an autonomous trading bot — which turns out to be its most valuable property.

The license reality

MIT — and that's not where the risk is.

MIT — full freedom

You can fork, rebrand, close your wedge and run it as a SaaS. There is no license constraint worth planning around here. The things that actually gate this product aren't in the code at all — they're regulatory and data-licensing, below.

How you'd productize it

One market, one research flow.

Take the generic engine and make it a vertical copilot for a specific exchange — say India's NSE/BSE:

  • A market-data adapter that swaps in that exchange's instruments and prices (the main build).
  • One plain-English flow — "explain this move, analyse this name, backtest this idea" — polished for a non-quant user.
  • Optionally a local model backend to control LLM cost and keep queries private.
  • Framing: keep it a research/education workspace end to end — it's the honest description and the compliant one.

The groove

Pin-and-wedge, concretely.

  1. Pin at a release (here, v0.1.19) — it's a young, fast-moving project, so treat interfaces as liable to churn.
  2. Deploy the app on a self-hosted PaaS; access-gate it.
  3. Build the data adapter for your exchange, starting with end-of-day / delayed data (cheaper and freer to redistribute).
  4. Wrap one research flow with clear disclaimers and no execution surface — no order routing, no buy/sell calls.

The catch

The regulator and the data vendor, not the license.

Research, not advice

In most markets, giving personalised investment advice or recommendations requires registration — for example India's SEBI research-analyst / investment-adviser regime. Stay strictly research and education: no personalised buy/sell/target calls, no execution. The upstream's own "research workspace, not a bot" stance is an asset here — keep it.

Market data is licensed

Real-time exchange feeds are paid and redistribution-restricted; end-of-day and delayed data are cheaper and more permissive. Build the MVP on EOD/delayed, and treat a live feed as a deliberate cost-and-license decision, not a default.
